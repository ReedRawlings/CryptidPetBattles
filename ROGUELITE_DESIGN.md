# Roguelite Mode — Design Document

## Overview

A separate game mode ("Expedition") using the same creatures and battle engine, but with a completely different progression flow. The existing arena mode stays untouched.

The player traverses a branching map of encounters across 3 acts, building their team along the way. A run ends when they defeat the Act 3 boss or lose all their lives.

---

## Run Structure

### Acts & Length

| Act | Nodes | Tier Pool | Boss |
|-----|-------|-----------|------|
| 1   | 5–6   | Tier 1–2  | Scripted boss team |
| 2   | 5–6   | Tier 2–3  | Scripted boss team |
| 3   | 5–6   | Tier 3–4  | Final boss team |

A full run is **15–18 encounters**. Each act ends with a mandatory boss node.

### Map

Branching paths — each node connects to 2–3 nodes in the next row. The player picks one path per step. Map is generated at the start of each act.

```
START ─┬─ [Battle] ─┬─ [Event] ──┬─ [Elite] ─── [BOSS]
       │             │             │
       └─ [Event] ──┼─ [Battle] ──┘
                     │
                     └─ [Shop] ───── [Battle] ── [BOSS]
```

Node types are distributed with constraints:
- Act boss is always the final node
- At least 1 shop node per act
- At least 1 elite per act
- No two elites in a row
- First node of Act 1 is always a battle (not elite)

---

## Node Types

### Battle
Standard fight against a generated opponent. Team size and star levels scale with depth (act + node position). On win, the player gets a **reward pick**: choose 1 of 3 from a mix of creatures, gold, or food.

### Elite
Harder fight — opponent has more creatures, higher stars, and tribal synergy. Drops a **relic** on victory (see Relics below). Losing an elite costs 2 lives instead of 1.

### Boss
Scripted team composition (not random). Each boss has a theme that tests specific counterplay. Defeating Act 3 boss wins the run. Losing costs 2 lives.

### Shop
Spend gold to buy creatures (3g), food (2g), or reroll (1g). Shop offers 4 creature slots + 2 food slots. The shop at Act 2+ includes higher tier creatures. No battle at shop nodes.

### Event
A choice between 2–3 options with risk/reward tradeoffs. Examples:
- **Mysterious Pond**: Heal 1 life OR upgrade a random creature to the next star level
- **Abandoned Den**: Gain a random Tier N+1 creature OR gain 5 gold
- **Tribal Shrine**: Transform a creature's tribe (keeps stats) OR gain 2 permanent ATK to your whole team
- **Gambler's Cave**: Bet 3 gold — win double or lose it

Events should never be purely negative. The worst outcome should be "you chose the less useful option."

### Rest
Heal 1 life OR remove a negative buff/relic OR upgrade (star-up) one creature that has enough experience. Rest nodes appear 1–2 times per act.

## Economy

### Gold
- **Starting gold**: 5 (just enough for 1 creature buy + 1 food or reroll)
- **Battle win reward**: 3–5 gold (scales with act)
- **Elite win reward**: 5–7 gold + relic
- **Battle loss**: 0 gold (you still lose a life)
- **Event nodes**: Sometimes grant gold as an option
- **No passive gold per turn** — gold comes from winning fights and events

### Creature Acquisition
- **Reward picks** after battles: choose 1 of 3 creatures/food/gold
- **Shop nodes**: Buy from a curated shop (gold-gated)
- **Events**: Sometimes offer creatures
- **No persistent shop between fights** — you prep your team with what you've gathered

### Team Persistence
- Team carries over between all fights
- Creatures do **not** auto-heal between fights — current HP persists
- Rest nodes and some events/food can heal creatures
- Buffs marked `permanent: true` persist; combat-only buffs reset between fights
- Star experience carries over (you can combine duplicates whenever you find them)

## Difficulty Scaling

### Per-Act Scaling

| Property | Act 1 | Act 2 | Act 3 |
|----------|-------|-------|-------|
| Opponent team size | 2–3 | 3–4 | 4–5 |
| Star probability (2★) | 0% | 20% | 35% |
| Star probability (3★) | 0% | 0% | 10% |
| Available tiers | 1–2 | 2–3 | 3–4 |
| Battle gold reward | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| Life loss on defeat | 1 | 1 | 2 |

### Elites
- Team size = act max + 1 (capped at 5)
- Guaranteed tribal synergy (at least 2 of one tribe)
- At least one 2★ creature (Act 2+: one 3★)

### Bosses
Scripted teams, not generated. Each boss should feel like a puzzle:
- **Act 1 Boss**: 3 creatures, all one tribe — tests whether the player can punch through a synergy
- **Act 2 Boss**: 4 creatures with a specific combo (e.g., tank + support backline + DoT)
- **Act 3 Boss**: 5 creatures, full synergy, high stars — the final test

Boss teams are predefined in data, not generated by `opponent.ts`.

---

## Relics

Relics are run-only passive modifiers gained from elite victories and some events. A player can hold up to 5 relics at a time. Relics last for the entire run.

### Example Relics

| Relic | Effect |
|-------|--------|
| Bone Charm | Spirit creatures gain +3 HP at the start of each battle |
| Fungal Spore | Flora creatures apply 1 stack of Poison on their first attack |
| Predator's Fang | Fauna creatures deal +2 damage on kill |
| Lucky Coin | Reward picks offer 4 options instead of 3 |
| Thick Hide | Your frontline creatures take 1 less damage per hit (min 1) |
| Second Wind | Once per battle, when a creature would faint, it survives with 1 HP |
| Gold Tooth | Gain +1 gold after each battle |
| Tribal Banner | Tribe synergy thresholds reduced by 1 (e.g., need 1 instead of 2 for first tier) |

Relics should be impactful enough to build around. "Win more" relics are fine — they reward the player for doing well at elites.

---

## Lives & Losing

- **Starting lives**: 3 (less forgiving than arena's 5 — runs should feel tense)
- **Life loss**: 1 per normal battle loss, 2 per elite/boss loss
- **Life recovery**: Rare — rest nodes, specific events, and one possible relic
- **Lives = 0**: Run ends in defeat. Player earns meta-currency based on progress.

---

## Meta-Progression (Between Runs)

### Unlock Currency: Essence

Earned at the end of every run based on how far the player got:

| Milestone | Essence |
|-----------|---------|
| Completed Act 1 | 10 |
| Completed Act 2 | 25 |
| Completed Act 3 (win) | 50 |
| Per elite defeated | 5 |
| Per relic held at run end | 3 |

### Unlockables

Unlockables expand the **pool** of what can appear, not direct power increases. This keeps early runs from feeling weak.

- **Creatures**: Start with the base pool (Tier 1–2). Unlock Tier 3–4 creatures for the pool over time.
- **Relics**: Start with 4–5 basic relics. Unlock more as you spend Essence.
- **Events**: Start with 3–4 basic events. Unlock more complex ones.
- **Starting bonuses** (optional): Spend Essence before a run for a one-time boost (e.g., start with 3 extra gold, start with a specific Tier 1 creature). These cost Essence per use, not permanent.

### Storage

`localStorage` under a separate key (`roguelite-meta`). Structure:

```typescript
interface RogueliteMeta {
  totalEssence: number;
  unlockedCreatureIds: string[];
  unlockedRelicIds: string[];
  unlockedEventIds: string[];
  stats: {
    totalRuns: number;
    totalWins: number;
    bestAct: number;
    totalElitesDefeated: number;
  };
}
```

## Technical Architecture

### What's Shared (No Changes)

- `src/game/battle.ts` — battle engine, used as-is
- `src/types/index.ts` — existing creature/buff/battle types
- `src/data/creatures.ts` + `creatures.json` — creature pool
- `src/data/foods.ts` — food items
- All existing UI components for battle rendering, creature cards, etc.

### What's New (Separate Files)

```
src/
  roguelite/
    types.ts              # RogueliteState, MapNode, Relic, Event, Act, etc.
    RogueliteContext.tsx   # Separate context + reducer for roguelite state
    map.ts                # Map generation (branching paths, node placement)
    encounters.ts         # Opponent generation tuned for roguelite scaling
    relics.ts             # Relic definitions and effect application
    events.ts             # Event definitions and resolution
    bosses.ts             # Scripted boss team data
    meta.ts               # Meta-progression (localStorage read/write)
  components/
    roguelite/
      ExpeditionMap.tsx   # Map UI (node grid, path selection)
      NodePreview.tsx     # Shows what's at the selected node
      RewardPick.tsx      # Post-battle reward selection (1 of 3)
      RelicBar.tsx        # Display active relics
      EventScreen.tsx     # Event choice UI
      RestScreen.tsx      # Rest node UI
      RunSummary.tsx      # End-of-run stats + Essence earned
```

### Game Mode Routing

The `Game.tsx` component (or a new wrapper) switches on `gameMode`:

```tsx
if (gameMode === 'roguelite') {
  return <Expedition />;  // Completely separate component tree
}
// Existing arena/versus flow unchanged
```

### Roguelite Phases

Separate from arena phases. The roguelite context manages its own phase:

```typescript
type RoguelitePhase =
  | 'map'           // Viewing map, picking next node
  | 'shop'          // At a shop node
  | 'battle'        // In combat (delegates to shared battle engine)
  | 'result'        // Post-battle, before reward
  | 'reward'        // Choosing reward pick
  | 'event'         // At an event node, making a choice
  | 'rest'          // At a rest node
  | 'bossIntro'     // Pre-boss flavor/preview
  | 'runComplete'   // Win or loss — show summary
```

### State Shape (Sketch)

```typescript
interface RogueliteState {
  phase: RoguelitePhase;
  player: Player;                    // Reuses existing Player type
  run: {
    seed: number;                    // For deterministic map gen
    act: number;                     // 1–3
    map: MapNode[][];                // 2D grid of nodes per act
    currentNodeIndex: [number, number]; // [row, col]
    visitedNodes: string[];          // Node IDs
    relics: Relic[];                 // Active relics (max 5)
    shopState: Shop | null;          // Only set at shop nodes
  };
  currentOpponent: Player | null;
  lastBattleResult: BattleResult | null;
  pendingRewards: RewardOption[];    // Available picks after battle
  meta: RogueliteMeta;              // Loaded from localStorage
}
```

## Open Questions

These are decisions that can wait until implementation but should be called out:

1. **Food system**: Food items exist in the data but the food system was recently removed/emptied. Does roguelite mode reintroduce food, or skip it?
2. **Creature healing between fights**: "No auto-heal" is harsh. Should there be a soft heal (e.g., heal 20% between fights)? Or is that what rest nodes are for?
3. **Duplicate creatures for combining**: How often should duplicates appear in reward picks? Combining is core to the base game — if it's too rare in roguelite, stars feel unattainable.
4. **Run length feel**: 15–18 nodes might feel long. Should there be a "short expedition" option (2 acts, ~10 nodes)?
5. **Seed sharing**: Should runs have shareable seeds so players can compare the same map/encounters?

---

## Summary

The roguelite mode is a self-contained system that shares creatures and combat but owns its own progression, state, and UI. The key pillars:

- **Branching map** with meaningful node-type choices
- **Scarce resources** — no free shop every turn, gold comes from winning
- **Relics** as run-defining power spikes from elites
- **Meta-progression** through pool expansion, not power creep
- **3 acts with scripted bosses** as milestone challenges

The arena mode continues to work exactly as it does today. The two modes share a creature pool and battle engine but nothing else.
